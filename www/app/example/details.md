The card you pressed was only a **pointer**. In this folder it is named `details.md`; from the previous page it was simply `details.md` again, because both sheets share a drawer.

Relative names are a courtesy. They keep the signature together when the whole gathering is moved to another shelf.

> A path with no slash stays in the room.
> A path that contains a slash leaves the room and names the building.

You can return with the back button. The root of the site has no such button: it is the street door, not a page in the gathering.
